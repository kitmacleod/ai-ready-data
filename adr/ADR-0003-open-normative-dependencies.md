# ADR-0003: Require openly readable normative dependencies

**Status:** Accepted for v0.2.0 Working Draft  
**Date:** 2026-11-04

## Context
The project aims to be open and independently implementable by environmental agencies, repositories, citizen-science organisations, researchers and software teams. Some relevant standards, notably ISO/IEC 5259, are copyright restricted and may require purchase. Others, including CF and many W3C/OGC/community specifications, are openly readable.

## Decision
All requirements necessary to implement or assess against this project must be stated openly in project material or through openly readable normative dependencies. Restricted standards may be informative references and may be crosswalked, but are not required for project conformance.

## Consequences
- ISO/IEC 5259 is informative rather than normative.
- The repository must not reproduce copyrighted normative text without permission.
- Alignment can be described conceptually and by public summaries.
