# Glossary

**Status:** v0.2.0 Working Draft. Terms will be aligned with existing community definitions where practical.

## AI-readiness
A relationship between a defined data product and an intended use, concerned with whether software and users can find, obtain, interpret, combine and rely on it with material limitations visible.

## Assessment target
The explicitly bounded object being assessed, including its version and scope. It may be narrower than a complete published dataset.

## Core conformance
Satisfaction of applicable common requirements independent of a specific use instance.

## Use-class conformance
Satisfaction of requirements associated with a broad task or mode of use.

## Specific-use fitness
The outcome of comparing an assessment target with explicit requirements for one intended use. It is not a general property of the dataset.

## Criterion
A single testable or reviewable requirement with one home dimension.

## Applicability
Whether a criterion applies to the assessment target and context.

## Evidence item
A traceable item used to support a criterion result, including its source, scope, date and method of acquisition.

## Evidence basis
The way evidence was obtained, such as deterministic inspection, structured metadata, documentation or responsible-person declaration.

## Finding / criterion result
The result of evaluating a criterion: met, partially met, not met, indeterminate, blocked, or not assessed.

## Assurance
How strongly a result is supported by its evidence. v0.2.0 prefers exposing the actual evidence basis rather than relying on an overall assurance label.

## Use requirement
A structured requirement for a specific intended use, such as daily temporal resolution, a stated geographical domain, or availability of uncertainty information.

## Use-specific verdict
Comparison of one use requirement with the target: supported, supported with caveats, not supported, cannot be determined, or requires domain judgement.

## Domain judgement
A conclusion requiring relevant scientific or operational expertise and not safely derivable only from machine inspection.

## Blocked
A criterion result indicating that assessment cannot proceed because a gating prerequisite has not been established.

## Nominal resolution
The encoded or published spacing/size of observations or grid cells.

## Effective resolution
The scale of information that the observation or production process can meaningfully support. This may differ from nominal resolution.

## Provenance
Information about sources, production, processing and responsible agents sufficient to understand how an assessment target came to exist.

## Quality and uncertainty information
Information supplied with or about data describing measurement quality, validation, limitations, uncertainty, bias, QC or similar characteristics. Its absence does not imply absence of uncertainty.

## Open / controlled / protected access
Provisional access categories. Access identifies who can obtain data; rights identify what authorised users may do with it. A protected dataset is not automatically less AI-ready.

## Improvement action
A documented action that can provide new or improved evidence, remove a blocker, mitigate a limitation or make a limitation clearer.

## Closing evidence
Evidence expected to demonstrate completion of an improvement action or permit reassessment.

## Evidence bundle
A versioned/snapshotted collection of evidence used as the input to deterministic evaluation.

## Assessment manifest
A machine-readable record of the target, evidence, specification/check versions, results, exclusions, overrides and other information needed to understand or replay an assessment.
